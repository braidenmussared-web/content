Your client didn't leave loud. They left quiet.

There are three signs before a coaching client disappears, and most coaches only spot them after the client's already gone.

Slower replies. A 24-hour turnaround becomes three days, and nobody mentions it.

Vague check-ins. "Yeah good thanks" replaces the real update they used to give you.

Missed sessions with no apology. The excuse that used to come with a skipped session just stops showing up.

None of these look like a red flag on their own. Together, they're a client quietly deciding to leave — usually about eight weeks before they actually cancel.

You can't watch for this in your head between fifteen other clients. It has to be something that watches for you.
